Loss Development Factor (LDF)
LDFj = Σ Cumulative Costj+1 / Σ Cumulative Costj
Measures the average multiplier factor as historical claims age from stage to stage.
The Chain Ladder Method
Future Estimate = Current Cumulative Cost × LDFj
